## Why this shape

* The camp-chair backrest is a mechanism unless the seat is structural: the straight side straps
  (FTIP→RTIP) and the crossed backstays are what make LC3 and LC5 stable. Remove the seat and the
  backrest folds — by design, that is how it packs.
* Forestays run straight to the same-side front dock so nothing crosses the rider's leg space.
* Tubes are 25 × 22 roll-wrapped CF for every member — one tube spec, one socket, one cutting list.
  Buckling SF ≥ 9 at 2.5 g in every class; the size is set by joint bearing and stiffness.

## Joiners

Generated by `cad/ps_cad.py` from the node geometry: a 21 mm core, one socket boss per tube
(33 mm OD, 50 mm deep, 4 mm wall, M5 cross pin), one anchor rod with a Ø6 mm cross hole per cord,
a T-slot shoe under each dock, a Ø28 × 55 mm domed peg on each seat tip. Print PA12-CF / PET-CF,
100 % infill, sockets oriented along the print axis where possible. Every joiner is proof-tested
(T-2, T-5) before it is marked.
