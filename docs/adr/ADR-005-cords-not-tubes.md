# ADR-005 — Dyneema cords close the truss; the seat is structural

Status: accepted · 2026-09

**Context.** A rigid chair frame needs many tubes and heavy joints. A camp chair gets its stiffness from the fabric.

**Decision.** Tubes only where compression is needed (rails, legs, poles, posts); Dyneema SK78 5 mm cords for every tension diagonal; the seat's side straps and edges are load-bearing members with their own rating and proof test.

**Consequences.** 0.74 kg of tube for the whole frame; the backrest is a mechanism without the seat (which is how it packs); cord and seat-edge safety factors (3.0–3.7) govern the rating rather than the tubes (≥ 9).

**Rejected.** All-tube space frame (+1.5 kg, more joiners); wire rope (heavier, kinks).
