# ADR-004 — 16 × 3.0 folding tubeless tire, packed deflated

Status: accepted · 2026-09

**Context.** Flotation on sand wants a big soft tire; the 62-inch bag wants a small one; the 50 lb limit wants a light one.

**Decision.** 16 × 3.0 (ISO 76-305) folding-bead tubeless. ~40 kPa ground pressure at PS-125, fits the 430 mm bag section only when deflated (Ø400).

**Consequences.** Excellent on playa, workable on dry sand at crawl speed. Packing needs a pump and tubeless reseating on site (T-11). Variants: PS-Lite 16 × 2.4 (lighter, worse sand), PS-Sand 20 × 4.0 (30 kPa, two-bag pack).

**Rejected.** 20 × 4.0 as baseline (breaks the one-bag rule); 12-inch solid tires (no flotation).
