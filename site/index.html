§01 · the constraint that designed the vehicle

It has to ride the bus.

The Burner Express bus to Black Rock City allows two luggage items per ticket, each at most 62 linear inches and 50 lb.1 Everything else — wheel size, battery split, tube count, the fact that the seat is structural — falls out of that one bag.

Legal shape: a mobility device

Seated electric scooters and golf carts are disallowed personal transport in Black Rock City.3 A four-wheeled seated electric vehicle is permitted only as an Accessibility Vehicle — an electric wheelchair or 3–4-wheel mobility scooter for a rider with a documented need.4 On Ocean Beach the ADA "other power-driven mobility device" route is the only one.7

So the Sprawler is designed, limited and documented as a powered mobility device (ISO 7176 / EN 12184 reference tests6): 8 km/h cap, firmware turn-rate limiter, lights, and a rating record a rider can hand to an inspector.

Two surfaces, one vehicle

Hard playa needs almost nothing (rolling resistance ≈ 0.02). Dry loose Ocean Beach sand needs ten times the drawbar and real flotation. The 16 × 3.0 tire lands at ~40 kPa ground pressure at the default class — excellent on playa, workable on dry sand at crawl speed — and only fits the bag section deflated. A 20 × 4.0 "PS-Sand" variant trades the one-bag pack for beach flotation.

§02 · what it is

A camp chair with a network.

From the sketch: one box — battery, motor, gearbox, brake, electronics — "4× redundant"; a chair frame like an REI camp chair with rods that stick into pockets on a canvas seat; two foot or hand controls; a signal bus; and a graph of nodes W W W W · B · C C · I 1…n · L 1…n.

Nodes

NodeCountWhat it does
W wheel module416 × 3.0 tubeless on a mini geared hub motor; 7s1p pack ( Wh); FOC controller; Ethernet node; printed carrier with a T-rail dock. The wheel module is the battery.

Three interfaces, frozen

Dock — fore/aft-symmetric T-rail + one drop pin; plus DATA (M12 D-coded, 100BASE-TX) and POWER/ID (M12 A-coded, SELV bus + a resistor that tells the node which dock it is in). Traction current never crosses a dock.

PS-Bus — UDP multicast JSON, no broker, no master. ctrl/tread/left at 50 Hz is both the command and the heartbeat; a wheel stops ms after the last valid one.

Seat pockets — four Ø32 mm cups over domed pegs on the frame tips. The seat is the only part never swapped, the most decorated, and — because its side straps close the truss — a rated structural member.

Skid steer: left command drives both left wheels, right drives both right. No steering mechanism is what makes the four modules identical. docs/02

§03 · evidence — every number below is computed by analysis/run_all.py

The frame is a truss, and the seat is part of it.

A 3D pin-jointed, tension-only truss solver runs four load cases for each rating class: static, 2.5 g vertical (bump/drop), 1.0 g + 0.6 g lateral skid, and a 0.5 W backrest lean. The camp-chair backrest is a mechanism until the seat's side straps and the crossed backstays are counted — so they are, and they are rated.

Stability

Turn-rate limiter

Firmware rule |ω|·|v| ≤ g with ω = (vR − vL)/track. The wheels enforce it because skid steer allows yaw the geometry cannot survive.

Range and drawbar by surface (default class, 8 km/h)

Usable energy Wh over four packs. Rolling-resistance coefficients are assumptions (hard playa 0.02, soft/dusty 0.06, wet sand 0.05, dry loose sand 0.20) until the dyno test corrects them. Dry loose sand at 10 % grade exceeds rated torque: the firmware derates speed there.

§04 · the safety code in the DNA

Every part carries a class. The vehicle is the weakest one.

Classes are payload masses: PS-100, PS-125, PS-150 kg (rider + gear). A part is rated by analysis with stated knock-downs and safety factors, then proof-loaded to 1.5 × static before it is marked. Firmware repeats the check: every node's hello carries its rating, and the vehicle drops to crawl if any live part is below the rider's class.

§05 · modules

Five things, each with one interface.

W — wheel module interface v1

, Ø mm inflated / packed, mm wide, target kg.

Mini geared hub motor 250 / 500 W, 20 / 35 N·m, no-clutch variant for regen and hill-hold. Printed carrier with aluminium torque plates; single-sided axle clamp (see caveat 2). docs/04

C — control module interface v1

One 25 mm rail clamp, the same two M12 connectors, one message: ctrl/tread/<side> {v: −1…1, seq} at 50 Hz. Return-to-centre, ≥ 5 % dead-band. The dock says CL or CR; the input form is the rider's business.

§06 · simulator — a port of firmware/sim/psbus_sim.py, same rules, same timings

Pull a wheel. Plug in the wrong one. Cut a cable.

Drive with the two treads, then break things. The supervisor only ever publishes a mode; every wheel decides for itself, from the messages it can see, whether it is allowed to move.

§07 · packing — the binding constraint, honestly

kg of . The margin is kg.

Four modules stack axially (4 × = mm of the 640 mm bag); tires must be deflated to fit the 430 mm section; tubes shock-cord into segments ≤ mm; cords, seat, controls and bus node fill the ~32 L of corner voids. The mass margin is 0.8 %: a target, not a measurement.

Mass budget vs 50 lb

If the scale disagrees

  1. Weigh every prototype part — the budget is derived from vendor masses.
  2. PS-Lite wheels (16 × 2.4) recover ~0.8 kg.
  3. Two-bag pack: the ticket allows two items. Modules in one, everything else in the other. PS-Sand (20 × 4.0) needs this anyway.
  4. Bus node and controls ride in the 9 × 10 × 17 in carry-on.
§08 · fleet operations — the reason for all the sameness

A camp keeps a rack of modules, not a row of vehicles.

Charging rack = docks

Same rail, same two connectors, bus-bar behind. Any module docks to charge at ≤ 3 A; 32 modules overnight ≈ 2.7 kWh. Slots publish SoC on the camp LAN.

Swap in under a minute

Stop → pull the pin → slide the module off the rail → slide the spare on → pin clicks → the node reads the dock and hellos with its rating → run, or crawl if the rating is low. Logged automatically.

Sign-out by class

Rider mass + gear → class. The steward assigns a vehicle rated at or above it and sets the rider class on the B node, which enforces it for the day. QR labels + hello logs answer "what was on that vehicle" in one query.

Lifecycle: new → proof-tested → in service → flagged → inspected → in service | retired. A flagged module never returns without an inspection entry. docs/11

§09 · staged rollout with go/no-go gates

Nothing rides until its stage passes.

0
One wheel module on a test rig. Motor, pack, node, carrier. Dyno for efficiency and torque; axle steel verified; T-2/T-3 socket and latch pull-outs.
GATE: drive efficiency ≥ 0.70 · latch holds 1.5 × LC2 · axle grade documented or double-sided clamp adopted
1
Frame kit + seat, static. Print the ten joiners, cut tubes, sew one seat. T-1 bend, T-4 cord/edge, T-5 proof at PS-125.
GATE: measured deflection within 10 % of prediction · no permanent set · proof passed on every joiner
2
Rolling prototype on hard ground. Four modules, two controls, B node. T-9 comms fail-safe, T-10 hot-swap timing, T-8 brake, T-14 tilt table.
GATE: every cable-pull stops the side ≤ 0.5 s · swap ≤ 60 s · crawl on a low-rated part · lift-off ≥ 24°
3
Pack test and bus rehearsal. T-11 pack/unpack ×3, checked scale, tubeless reseat with a mini pump.
GATE: ≤ 50 lb on the scale · ≤ 15 min each way · tires reseat
4
Surface trials. Ocean Beach (as an OPDMD, with permission checked) and playa-equivalent: range loop, T-12 dust, T-13 thermal.
GATE: hard-ground range ≥ 15 km · no ingress · motor < 95 °C on the grade loop
5
Fleet of four with a rack. Sign-out by class, swap drills, logs, incident review. Then publish v1.0 of the interfaces.
GATE: 30 fleet-days without a rating bypass or an unlogged swap
§10 · caveats — read before building

What is idealised, unverified, or marginal.

  1. The 50 lb margin is 0.19 kg. Expect PS-Lite wheels or the two-bag pack on the first prototypes.
  2. Axle steel is assumed (785 MPa). SF 2.1 at PS-125 depends on it; otherwise the carrier must support the axle on both sides.
  3. The truss is idealised: pin joints, tension-only cords, seat as five straight members, no joint compliance. Deflections are lower bounds.
  4. Printed allowables are a 0.5 guess on datasheet values until T-2/T-5 data exist for the chosen filament and printer.
  5. Range assumes 0.70 drive efficiency and textbook rolling-resistance coefficients. Dry-sand range (2.5 km) is a warning.
  1. Deflated-tire packing is the only way the 16 × 3.0 fits. Tubeless reseating on the playa must be proven.
  2. Regulatory: BRC only as an Accessibility Vehicle for a rider with a documented need; Ocean Beach only as an OPDMD. Nothing here is a permit.
  3. Hot-swap while rolling is a demonstrated protocol behaviour, not a procedure. Stop first.
  4. Vendor variance in hub-motor axles, wire exits and gear ratios — the carrier is parametric for that reason.
  5. No prototype exists. Everything here is analysis, CAD and simulation. Stage 0 is a single module on a rig.
